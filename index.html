MODEL v1.0

A small model, with a defined scope.

This demonstrator evaluates a fitted surrogate for a fixed battery-pack geometry. It calculates a new field for any supported input combination; it does not select a stored temperature image.

Two inputs

Inlet mass-flow parameter: 0.005–0.020 kg/s.
Coolant inlet temperature: 25–35°C.

Fixed charging condition

4C constant-current charging, initial SOC 10%, nominal endpoint SOC 80%, and a 630 s endpoint. Initial battery and ambient temperatures are both 35°C.

What the model returns

Two internal XY cut planes at z = 86.5 mm and z = 8.5 mm. Each has a 161 × 129 grid. Peak and mean values refer to these sampled cut planes, not the whole battery volume.

Validation

77 training cases fit the mean, POD basis and RBF weights. 20 validation cases select RBF settings; 20 independent test cases measure error. This is validation against COMSOL, not physical experiments.

How a prediction is made

Flow is log-transformed, inlet temperature is linearly transformed, and both are mapped to [−1, 1]. The 77 Gaussian kernels use ε = 0.5. Their weighted sums predict five POD coefficients, which reconstruct both fields from the training-set mean and spatial modes.

u₁ = 2 ln(ṁ / 0.005) / ln(4) − 1
u₂ = 2 (Tᵢₙ − 25) / 10 − 1
κⱼ = exp(−0.5 ‖u − uⱼ‖²)
aᵢ = Σⱼ κⱼ wⱼᵢ

Coverage matters

Inputs outside the supported ranges are rejected. At the minimum-flow edge, 25°C and 35°C fall outside the training-case convex envelope because three earlier cases were excluded. A boundary notice appears for these corner regions. No generalization to a different geometry or charging strategy is claimed.

The separate five-input, 100%-SOC dataset is not used by this version. Ambient temperature, initial SOC and C-rate remain fixed here.
