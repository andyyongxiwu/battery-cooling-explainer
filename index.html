Five-input endpoint model

Inputs and endpoint

Flow: 0.005–0.020 kg/s. Inlet temperature: 25–35°C. Initial SOC: 10–60%. Charging rate: 1–5C. Ambient temperature: 10–40°C. Battery initial temperature equals ambient. The endpoint is SOC 100%, at t = 3600 × (1 − SOC₀) / C-rate seconds. This is a charging endpoint, not steady state.

Prediction

The model learns temperature rise above the coolant inlet. A training-only mean rise, the current inlet temperature and 24 POD mode contributions reconstruct two 161 × 129 fields. Gaussian RBF uses 230 centers, ε = 0.25, ridge = 0.0001 and an affine term. The fan draws 77 representative centers; all 230 are evaluated. Five modes are displayed at a time; all 24 are used by default.

Data and validation

304 complete cases: 230 training, 34 validation and 40 independent test cases. The planned GTR011–GTR020 and GVA005–GVA010 exports are missing and excluded. Actual endpoint SOC was audited for all included cases. Model selection used validation only. Test RMSE: 0.07784 K; largest point error: 0.26598 K.

Scope

Geometry and nominal cell capacity are fixed. Predictions accept new continuous combinations in the stated ranges. Reported errors compare against COMSOL, not experiments, and do not guarantee accuracy at every new input. Peak and mean values describe the internal cut planes, not the entire battery volume.
